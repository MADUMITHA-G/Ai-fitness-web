Muscle Building Workouts

Powerful No-Equipment Routines to Build Strength and Mass

Push-Ups

Builds chest, shoulders, and triceps. Do 3 sets of 15 reps.

Bodyweight Squats

Develops lower body muscles like quads and glutes. 3 sets of 20 reps.

Plank Hold

Strengthens your core and stabilizers. Hold 45 seconds × 3 sets.

Lunges

Targets hamstrings and glutes. 10 reps per leg × 3 rounds.

Burpees

Full-body exercise for strength and stamina. 3 sets of 10 reps.

Mountain Climbers

Activates core and shoulders while burning fat. 30 seconds × 3 rounds.

Tricep Dips

Tones arms and chest. Perform 3 sets of 12 reps using a chair.

Superman Hold

Builds lower back and glutes. Hold for 20 seconds × 3 sets.

Bicycle Crunches

Strengthens abs and obliques. 3 sets of 20 reps.
