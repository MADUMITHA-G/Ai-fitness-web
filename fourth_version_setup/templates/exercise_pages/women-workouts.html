Workouts for Women

Simple No-Equipment Exercises for Strength and Toning

Bodyweight Squats

Strengthens thighs and glutes. Perform 3 sets of 15 reps.

Glute Bridge

Targets hips and lower back. Hold for 20 seconds × 3 rounds.

Leg Raises

Tones your lower abs. 3 sets of 12 reps.

Plank Hold

Improves posture and core stability. Hold for 30 seconds × 3 rounds.

Side Leg Lifts

Tones outer thighs and hips. 3 sets of 15 reps per leg.

Jumping Jacks

Great for warm-up and cardio. 3 rounds of 40 seconds.

Tricep Dips

Tones upper arms. Use a sturdy chair. 3 sets of 10 reps.

High Knees

Improves endurance and agility. 3 sets of 30 seconds.

Superman Pose

Strengthens back and glutes. Hold for 20 seconds × 3 rounds.
