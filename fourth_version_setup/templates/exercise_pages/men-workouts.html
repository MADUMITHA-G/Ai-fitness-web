Workouts for Men

Effective No-Equipment Workouts

Wall Push-Ups

Great for chest, triceps, and shoulders. Do 3 sets of 15 reps.

Bodyweight Squats

Targets legs and glutes. Perform 20 reps.

Plank

Strengthens your core. Hold for 45 seconds × 3 rounds.

Lunges

Improves balance and leg strength. 10 reps each leg × 3 sets.

Burpees

Full-body fat burner. 3 sets of 12 reps.

Mountain Climbers

Builds endurance and core strength. 3 sets of 30 seconds.

Jumping Jacks

Boosts cardio and warms up the body. 3 rounds of 40 seconds.

Tricep Dips

Tones your arms. Use a chair or bench. 3 sets of 10 reps.

Crunches

Targets abs and obliques. 3 sets of 20 reps.
